The new look, next to today's

Each block shows Now (today's styles, condensed from packages/ui) above New (docs/05-design/visual-refresh.md). The structure, order and texts are identical; only the look and motion change. Hover, click and tab through everything: the category bar opens its menu, the Buying/Selling switch slides, the buttons react, and the admin colour picker works with the real colour function.

Use the toolbar to switch Light/Dark, Reduced motion (what users who turn off animations see), Phone 360 width, and New only.

The Owner's example. Today the categories are plain grey text with an underline. New: each top-level category is a pill in its own colour with a dot. 9 · Category colours: starter palette and derived shades

The admin only picks the base colour; every other shade is calculated, so text stays readable in light and dark (always ≥ 4.5:1, see the "Text on solid" column). S1–S5 are spares for new categories.
