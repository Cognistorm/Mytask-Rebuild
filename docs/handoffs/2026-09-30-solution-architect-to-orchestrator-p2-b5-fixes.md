## Owner questions (for the orchestrator to number; my recommendation for each)
1. **Payout cooling period (SEC-05; also SEC-06).**
   *Question:* If someone gets into an account and changes the email address, the password or the bank account (IBAN), they could withdraw the money at once. Should withdrawals pause for a short time after such a change?
   *Options:* (a) No pause (as today). (b) Pause new withdrawals for 24 h after an IBAN, email or password change, including changes made by staff. (c) Pause for 72 h. (d) No pause, but mark the withdrawal "payout details changed on …" for the approving staff member.
   *Recommendation:* (b) plus (d). 24 h is short enough for real users and gives the account owner time to react to the "your details changed" email. Also confirm the technical part already designed: accounts without a password confirm email, IBAN and "log out other sessions" with an emailed code.
   *Hook:* additive later. The check lives in `createWithdrawal` (new `422` reason), and a new nullable field `payoutDetailsChangedAt` goes on `AdminWithdrawal`. No contract change is needed now.
2. **Extra re-authentication for risky staff actions (SEC-06, SEC-07).**
   *Question:* Some staff actions can take over a user's account, or weaken security, without the staff member re-entering their password. Should these also ask for the password (or code) again?
   *Actions:* changing a user's email; switching off a user's 2FA; changing the settings for staff 2FA, 2FA, reCAPTCHA, login limits and account activation (S-052, S-053, S-056…S-064, S-124); changing plan prices; creating a 100% promo code.
   *Options:* (a) Keep the current list (spec 16 AC-7). (b) Add the user email change, the 2FA switch-off and the security settings. (c) Also add plan prices and 100%/large promo codes. Separately: always notify the user when staff switch off their 2FA (NEW email)?
   *Recommendation:* (c) and yes to the notification. It costs staff one password entry per 15 minutes.
   *Hook:* additive. Add `stepUp: true` / extend `stepUpFor`; `403 REAUTH_REQUIRED` already exists.
3. **Who sees full bank account numbers (SEC-08 = Q-113).**
   *Question:* Today every Customer Support agent would see the full IBAN and name of everyone who withdraws. Should that be limited?
   *Options:* (a) Keep it (`payments.read`). (b) Only staff who approve withdrawals (`withdrawals.approve`) see the withdrawals list and detail. (c) Support sees a masked IBAN (`GE•• … 1234`); only approvers see the full one, and every full view is logged.
   *Recommendation:* (b). It is the simplest, and support does not need IBANs.
   *Hook:* a change of `x-permission.permission` on `adminListWithdrawals` / `adminGetWithdrawal`, or a masked field. Additive.
4. **Third-party scripts on public pages (SEC-11, extends Q-085).**
   *Question:* Custom scripts (for example Google Tag Manager) run on the same site where users are logged in. A script from an outside company could act as any logged-in visitor (read messages, pay from the wallet). Do you accept that risk for the script hosts you allow, and may "tag manager" services (whose content can be changed outside our control) be allowed?
   *Options:* (a) Accept any host the Super-admin lists. (b) Allow only fixed-code vendors (analytics pixels), no tag managers, with versions pinned where possible. (c) (b) now, and in Phase 3 study running custom code in an isolated frame.
   *Recommendation:* (c).
   *Hook:* ADR-013 §7 already documents the trust boundary and the kill switch (S-110 OFF). A host ban list or iframe isolation can be added without contract changes.
5. **How long personal data and ID documents are kept (SEC-12; legal, Georgian Law on Personal Data Protection).**
   *Question:* How long do we keep ID photos and selfies after a verification decision? What happens to the personal data of deleted accounts? How do we handle a user who asks for a copy of their data or its deletion?
   *Options:* e.g. delete KYC images 30/90/180 days after the decision (keep the decision record); anonymise deleted accounts after N years (keep money and invoice records as tax law requires); handle requests with a staff tool or a documented manual process.
   *Recommendation:* KYC images deleted 90 days after the decision; deleted accounts pseudonymised after the tax-retention period (confirm the period with your accountant); a manual process at launch, documented for staff; old storage versions expire with the same rule. Must be decided before the Phase 5 KYC import.
   *Hook:* data-model gets retention jobs and columns (a purge date on KYC file rows, an anonymised-at marker on users) and ADR-009 a lifecycle rule. Additive; the architect adds them after the answer.
6. **Cash-out of card top-ups (SEC-13).**
   *Question:* A stolen card could top up a wallet and then withdraw the money to a bank account. The chargeback lands on MyTask. Should money added by card top-up be limited?
   *Options:* (a) No limit (today). (b) Top-up money can only be spent on MyTask, never withdrawn. (c) It can be withdrawn only after N days, and only to an IBAN whose holder name matches. (d) No rule, but the approving staff member sees where the balance came from (top-ups vs earnings).
   *Recommendation:* (d) at launch as a minimum, plus (c) with N = 14 days if you accept the extra rule.
   *Hook:* additive. A source-of-funds breakdown field on `adminGetWithdrawal`, and a new `422` reason in `createWithdrawal` if (b)/(c).
7. **Referral limits (SEC-18).**
   *Question:* Each referred sign-up earns 10 points, and 10 referrals = 1 month of Premium. Someone could create fake accounts (social login creates active accounts at once). Should there be limits?
   *Options:* (a) No limit. (b) A monthly cap per referrer (e.g. 20). (c) Credit only after the referred user verifies the email or makes a first paid order. (d) Flag referrals from the same device or IP for staff.
   *Recommendation:* (b) + (d); (c) with "first paid order" if abuse appears.
   *Hook:* everything goes into the one `ReferralService.creditSignup` (item 11). No contract change except an optional flag on the admin referral list.
8. **"Read" permissions that change something (SEC-21).**
   *Question:* Staff who may only *view* payments can also mark a payment "reviewed", mark a reconciliation difference "reviewed", or ask BOG for a payment's status. Staff who may only *view* system health can retry a failed email. Is that acceptable?
   *Options:* (a) Accept as is (low risk: bookkeeping flags; the BOG check only runs the verified path). (b) Add a new permission `payments.review` for the three payment actions.
   *Recommendation:* (a), recorded as accepted. The risk is low, and a new permission changes the approved catalogue (P-114).
   *Hook:* if (b), it is a catalogue addition plus an `x-permission` change on three operations. Additive.
